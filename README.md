![Bountiful Fares](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f7ff77b556f36b179d51d74b3cb8eadcfc148522.png)

**Bountiful Fares expands upon existing mechanics and adds simple, new content to the food and farming aspect of Minecraft, while still being faithful to the base game.** Please note that features may be added or changed in the future.
<br>

<details>
  <summary>PLEASE READ IF UPDATING TO VERSION 3.0.0+!</summary>
Updating from a version before 3.0.0 to one after will break a few things in your world:

**Trellises** - trellis blocks with plants may disappear. It is recommended to break the plants on them before updating.

**Tea Blends** - tea blend items have been removed and their functionality has been replaced by the items they are made of.

**Custard** - custard items have been removed.
</details>
<br>
<br>

![Features](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/0418a9c6cba2880676b82f869e5098d41d578819.png)

#### - Fruit trees - for Apples, Oranges, Lemons, Plums, and Coconuts
#### - Wild crops and new crop types - such as Passion Fruit, Leeks, and Spongekins
#### - Trellises - for decoration or growing vine crops
#### - New ancient plants from Sniffers - Hoary Apple Trees and Lapisberries
#### - Ceramic blocks - which can be dyed any color
#### - New food effects - adds some extra complexity to food
#### - Fermentation - unlocks new recipes like Mead or Cider
#### - Many new food items and blocks
#### - New paintings - featuring content from the mod
##

![Frequently asked questions](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7083d0b815d527fb670ba807c3c4f793662463c7.png)

### Is this a Farmer's Delight addon?
No, this is an independent mod.

### Will there be a Bedrock version? / Can I port Bountiful Fares to Bedrock?
No, don't expect anything Bedrock-related, and please do not port it yourself.

### Will there be a port to [insert past version] / Can I port Bountiful Fares to [insert past version]?
No, some features require the latest versions, and please do not port it yourself.

### What versions will Bountiful Fares be updated to?
Currently, only the 1.20.1 and 1.21.1 versions are being updated, its unlikely anything past that will get a port.

### Can I use Bountiful Fares in a modpack/server/other content?
Absolutely!

### Can I use Bountiful Fares's models/textures for a separate project?
No, please don't use any of it's models/textures for anything else, even with credit. (Check license on github)

### Can I use Bountiful Fares's assets for an addon?
In short, yes! But please check the license on gitub.
