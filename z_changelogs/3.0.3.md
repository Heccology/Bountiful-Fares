# Bountiful Fares Version 3.0.3

## IMPORTANT - WHEN UPDATING TO VERSION 3.0.0+

This mod now requires Nexuslib.

**Updating from a version before 3.0.0 to one after will break a few things in your world:**

**Trellises** - trellis blocks with plants may disappear. It is recommended to break the plants on them before updating.

**Tea Blends** - tea blend items have been removed and their functionality has been replaced by the items they are made of.

**Custard** - custard items have been removed.

## Compatibility
- Updated compatibility with Frontiers (Fabric)
  - Added Blighted Birch trellises and pickets

## Tweaks
- Some small tweaks to Cup & Water Cups
  - Empty cups can now take water from water blocks when dispensed
  - Empty cups can now take water from a Cauldron, & water cups can put water in
  - Water cups will now turn Dirt into Mud, both when used by hand and when dispensed
- Renamed "Hoary/Walnut Boat Chest" to "Hoary/Walnut Boat with Chest" to match Vanilla names
- Changed "Jack O' Straw" to "Jack o'Straw" to match Vanilla names
