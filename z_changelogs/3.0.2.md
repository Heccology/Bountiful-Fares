# Bountiful Fares Version 3.0.2

## IMPORTANT - WHEN UPDATING TO VERSION 3.0.0+

This mod now requires Nexuslib.

**Updating from a version before 3.0.0 to one after will break a few things in your world:**

**Trellises** - trellis blocks with plants may disappear. It is recommended to break the plants on them before updating.

**Tea Blends** - tea blend items have been removed and their functionality has been replaced by the items they are made of.

**Custard** - custard items have been removed.

## Fixes
- Fixed hanging Golden Apples culling neighboring blocks
- Fixed loading any world stalling when both Bountiful Fares and any mod that uses Surveyor are installed
- Fixed(?) a crash relating to furnace fuels on neoforge
- Fixed Trellises not playing block placement sounds
